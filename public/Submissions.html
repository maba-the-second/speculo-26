Competition Guidelines

Submission Rules

  • Each participant can submit up to 3 photographs per category.
  • You may enter multiple categories.
  • All photographs must be original work.
  • Submit high-resolution images (minimum 3000px on longest side).
  • File format: JPEG or PNG.

Image Requirements

  • Basic editing is allowed (crop, exposure, color correction).
  • No composite images or heavy manipulation.
  • For monochrome category: Shades of black and white only.
  • For mobile category: captured with smartphone or tablet.

Judging Criteria

  • Technical excellence and composition.
  • Creativity and originality.
  • Impact and emotional resonance.
  • Adherence to category theme.
  • Overall artistic merit.
