Legacy of Visual Storytelling

Founded in 2003, the Photographic Society of Dharmaraja College has been dedicated to promoting photography among young enthusiasts through national events, competitions, livestreams, workshops, and educational activities, helping students develop their creative vision and technical mastery.

Sri Lanka’s Premier All-Island Photography Competition

Speculo is the society’s signature all-island photography competition and exhibition, providing Sri Lankan photographers with a powerful platform. The event features hands-on masterclasses by renowned photographers and a live photography photowalk capturing the historic beauty of Sri Lanka.
